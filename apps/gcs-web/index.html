Tell the drones (optional)

Type a change in plain words. The AI (or the built-in rules) only turns it into change requests; a fixed-rule check accepts or refuses each one, and you decide whether to apply them. The AI never plans routes or decides safety.

2 · Plan with CBBA

Each drone is an agent: it bids for places, talks to the drones it can hear, and they share the work with no repeats. Then a safety check (fixed rules, no AI) approves or blocks the plan. Needs the fleet-agent server on port 8020.

4 · Live agents (simulated flight)
